1. What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device by your web browser. They can be used to remember preferences or enable login sessions. This website does not use cookies for advertising, analytics, tracking, or profiling purposes.

2. Cookies used on this site

CookiePurposeDurationType
sessionAdmin authentication and login sessionSession (deleted on browser close) or up to 8 hoursStrictly necessary

No other cookies are set by this website. Specifically, we do not use analytics, marketing, advertising, social media, or third-party tracking cookies of any kind.

Public statistics are generated only as aggregate server-side counters, without visitor IDs, IP hashes, User-Agent hashes, full referrers, browser fingerprinting, tracking pixels, localStorage analytics, or third-party analytics services.

3. Session cookie (admin only)

A session cookie named session is set only when an administrator logs into the dashboard area. This cookie is strictly necessary for the functioning of the administrative interface. It stores a cryptographically signed session identifier that expires when the browser is closed or after 8 hours of inactivity, whichever comes first. It is never used for any other purpose.

Anonymous visitors to the public website do not receive any cookies.

4. Local storage (UI preference)

The public website does not use localStorage, session storage, tracking pixels, browser fingerprinting, or third-party analytics.

5. Third-party services

This website does not embed external scripts, widgets, or resources from third-party services. All assets (stylesheets, JavaScript libraries, fonts, icons) are hosted on the same server. No external domain is contacted when you browse this website.

6. Managing cookies

You can control and delete cookies through your browser settings. Most browsers allow you to block all cookies, delete existing cookies, or receive a notification before a cookie is stored. Note that blocking the session cookie will make the admin dashboard inaccessible.

7. Changes to this policy

Any changes to this Cookie Policy will be posted on this page. The date of the latest revision is indicated at the bottom of this document.

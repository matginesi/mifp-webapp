# Code of Conduct

**1. Respect and Dignity** — All participants in MIFP activities must treat each other with respect and dignity. We are committed to providing a welcoming and supportive environment for all, regardless of background, identity, or status. Harassment, discrimination, or disrespectful behavior of any kind will not be tolerated.

**2. Inclusivity** — MIFP embraces diversity in all its forms. We actively promote the participation of scientists from all countries, cultures, genders, and career stages. Our events and activities should reflect the rich diversity of the international physics community and create opportunities for underrepresented groups.

**3. Professional Integrity** — All MIFP members are expected to uphold the highest standards of professional integrity in their research and interactions. This includes honest reporting of results, proper attribution of others' work, and transparent disclosure of conflicts of interest. Scientific misconduct, including plagiarism and data fabrication, is grounds for immediate exclusion.

**4. Collaborative Spirit** — MIFP exists to foster collaboration, not competition. Members should actively seek opportunities to share knowledge, support colleagues, and build productive partnerships. Constructive criticism should be offered respectfully, and all participants should feel safe to express ideas and ask questions.

**5. Safe Environment** — MIFP is committed to providing a safe environment at all its events and in all its activities. This means zero tolerance for harassment, intimidation, or any form of abuse. All participants have the right to feel safe, and any concerns should be reported to the designated MIFP contact persons.

**6. Environmental Responsibility** — We recognize our responsibility to minimize the environmental impact of our activities. MIFP encourages sustainable practices at events, promotes virtual participation when possible, and supports initiatives that reduce the carbon footprint of international scientific collaboration.

**7. Responsible Communication** — Members should communicate about science and MIFP activities responsibly, avoiding sensationalism and ensuring accuracy. Public statements should be clearly distinguished from personal opinions, and members should be mindful of the impact their communications may have on the broader community.

**8. Enforcement** — Violations of this Code of Conduct should be reported to the MIFP leadership. All reports will be treated confidentially and investigated promptly. Consequences for violations may range from a warning to exclusion from MIFP activities and membership revocation, depending on the severity of the offense.

## Our Commitment

By participating in MIFP activities, you agree to abide by this Code of Conduct. We are all responsible for creating and maintaining a respectful, inclusive, and productive scientific community. When we each do our part, the entire community benefits.

*The Mediterranean Institute of Fundamental Physics*

# How to Become a Sponsor

The Mediterranean Institute of Fundamental Physics (MIFP) invites organizations and institutions to support its mission through sponsorship.

## Why Sponsor MIFP?

Sponsoring MIFP provides visibility within the international physics community, supports open scientific exchange, and contributes to cutting-edge research in fundamental physics.

## Sponsorship Opportunities

We offer a range of sponsorship packages that can be tailored to your organization's goals and budget.

For inquiries about current opportunities and customized packages, please contact us at [info@mifp.eu](mailto:info@mifp.eu).

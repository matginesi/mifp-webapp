# About MIFP

The Mediterranean Institute of Fundamental Physics (MIFP) is a private, independent, non-governmental and non-profit institution created to unite scientists working in different countries and across different areas of physics.

According to the institute profile published on Wikipedia, MIFP was established in 2010 and is based in Marino, Rome, Italy. Its purpose is to support international scientific collaboration, teaching programmes, workshops, schools and conferences, with a particular focus on fundamental physics and the Mediterranean research community.

MIFP works to reduce the administrative and logistical burden around research activities, making it easier for members to collaborate on projects, grant applications and scientific events. The institute also promotes physics education and international exchange through meetings and schools connected to nanophotonics, photovoltaics and light-matter coupling.

The institute's motto is _Dubium sapientiae initium. Memento audere semper_.

Source: [Mediterranean Institute of Fundamental Physics, Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mediterranean_Institute_of_Fundamental_Physics).

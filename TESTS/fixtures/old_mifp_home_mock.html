Congratulations to Prof. Alexey Kavokin and Dr. Stella Kavokina on their Science Perspective

Their paper "Parisi scaling and anomalous decay of exciton-polaritons in a photonic crystal" has been published in Science. The work demonstrates how polariton condensation offers a nonequilibrium platform to test critical exponents.

Congratulazioni from all of us at MIFP!

Congratulation to Dr. Nikita Kavokine

Dr. Nikita Kavokine has been awarded the PRISM-2024 prize for his outstanding contributions to the field of quantum photonics. The award ceremony will take place at the MIFP headquarters.

PLMNC 2023 - Medellin, Colombia

The next PLMNC conference will be held in Medellin, Colombia, organized by Prof. Javiera Londoño from Universidad de la Amazonia. Topics include polariton lattices, Bose-Einstein condensation, and nonlinear optics.

ICP2DC6 - 2022, Yerevan Armenia

The 6th International Conference on Physics of 2D Crystals was held in Yerevan, Armenia, hosted by Quantum College. The event featured talks on transition metal dichalcogenides and van der Waals heterostructures.

The image shows a Bose-Einstein condensate of exciton-polaritons.

MIFP and University of Amazonia Join Forces in Promoting Scientific Excellence

MIFP is proud to announce a new collaborative agreement with Universidad de la Amazonia in Florencia, Colombia. The partnership will focus on joint research projects, student exchanges, and academic events in the field of fundamental physics.
